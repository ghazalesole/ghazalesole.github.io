Which retail customers should a retention team focus on?

I used purchasing behavior to separate customers a retailer might want to win back from customers it could focus on retaining.

Business question

A customer who spent heavily last year and then stopped buying calls for a different offer from someone who buys regularly. How could a retailer distinguish those groups in transaction data?

Data and method

I used the UCI Online Retail transaction dataset and summarized positive purchases for each customer. I measured how recently they bought, how often they bought, and how much they spent. I transformed and scaled those measures before fitting K-means models.

I compared two through ten clusters. Two scored highest on the silhouette measure, but four gave me more distinct groups to interpret for a retention campaign.

Results

The model assigned all 4,338 customers to four segments. The largest was the inactive group, with 1,552 customers. The 727 loyal high-value customers made up about one in six.

Business implication

I would test a win-back offer with inactive customers and a loyalty offer with the high-value group. A holdout group in each segment would let the retailer measure whether either offer changed repeat purchasing.
