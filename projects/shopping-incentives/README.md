# Shopping incentives and purchase intention

For my 2021 master’s thesis at Kharazmi University, I used interviews and a survey to study the benefits of game-like features in Iranian online stores. I used SmartPLS to examine relationships with stated purchase intention.

In a separate 2022 publication, my coauthors and I used qualitative interviews to identify four types of benefits. The case study distinguishes the thesis from that paper and links the published findings.
