Consumer research · SmartPLS

How shoppers value game-like features in online stores

I studied how the benefits of game-like features in Iranian online stores related to shoppers’ stated intention to buy.

Research question

Online stores often give shoppers a reason to play, compete, or earn a reward. I wanted to understand how the benefits of these features related to people’s intention to buy in Iranian online stores.

Data and method

For my master’s thesis at Kharazmi University, I used interviews and analyzed survey responses from 312 people. I checked the survey measures for reliability using Cronbach’s alpha. I then used partial least squares structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM) in SmartPLS to examine how the benefits related to purchase intention.

Results

I used the findings to develop customer-engagement recommendations for online retailers. A related paper that I coauthored identified four types of benefits that retailers can design for.

Business implication

The survey measured purchase intention. To see whether a feature changes actual purchases, I would test it with a group of shoppers and compare checkout completion with a control group.
